# Stellata

## Rotonda

From Palladio's Villa La Rotonda: a domed hall in the center, and four identical porticos, one on each side, all leading into it. No side is the front.

## Pavilion

- **Three — unit.** Complete in itself.
- **Six — structure.** Holds units.
- **Nine — whole.** Holds structures, and reaches each unit only through them.

Things are created from below and held from above. The numbers mark an order, not a count, and are relative, not fixed: a three is the nine of what it holds, and a nine is the three of something larger. The same order holds at every size.

## Plan and section

Two drawings describe how parts relate within a thing. The plan shows their roles around the center; the section shows their levels: what holds each part and what it holds.
