About

Hey, I'm Ola Jin.

I studied architecture in Prague, where I spent five years learning how to think about space, people, and why the cities we live in feel the way they do. More than anything, architecture gave me a way of seeing the world.

As I finished my studies, a difficult job market pushed me to rethink where I wanted to take my skills and what I wanted to build.

Over time, I found myself increasingly drawn toward the way code feels like digital DNA. How pieces fit together, just in a different form.

I'm currently a student, figuring things out one project at a time. If you're building something interesting, feel free to reach out.
